# RightNote Travel

A small installable phone app for paying in cash abroad. Type a price in Vietnamese đồng, see it in Australian (or US) dollars, and get told which notes to hand over.

**Open or install:** https://magnus-eriksson-k.github.io/rightnote-travel/

## Features

- **Convert** đồng to AUD or USD as you type. The keypad does sums, so `45000 + 120000` works.
- **Price-tag shorthand** under the amount, the way Vietnamese prices are written (`200k`, `1.5 triệu`).
- **How to pay** shows up to three ways to make up the amount:
  - **Fewest notes**, for the exact amount.
  - **Without the biggest note**, e.g. no 500k notes, which suits vendors short on change.
  - **Round up**, with the change you should get back. This only appears when it saves notes.
- **Note cards** show a photo of each note, what's on the back and its colour, plus a button to load that amount. Each note has its own link, e.g. `…/rightnote-travel/#n500k`.
- **Works offline** once installed.
- **Rates refresh** automatically once a day when online, or with **Refresh rate**. You can also type your own rate, e.g. your bank's, and **Reset rate** goes back to the market rate.

## Install

Open the link once while online, then:

- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari only):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

After that the app opens from its own icon and works without internet. Without a connection it uses the last saved rate, and the date at the top shows how old that is.

## Sharing

Send people the link above. Each person's rates and settings stay on their own phone.

## Notes

- Rates are mid-market. Cash and card rates in Vietnam usually sit 1–2% either side.
- The note photos were taken by the owner and are embedded in the app.
- The site is public: anyone with the link can open it.
